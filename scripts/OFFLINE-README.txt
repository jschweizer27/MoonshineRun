SHINE - A Prohibition Bootlegging Demo
=======================================

HOW TO PLAY
  1. Double-click Shine.html. It opens in your web browser.
  2. Click START THE RUN (or press Enter).

  No installation and no internet connection needed.
  Works best in the latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

CONTROLS
  W / Up arrow       throttle
  S / Down arrow     brake and reverse
  A D / Left Right   steer
  Space              handbrake

THE LOOP
  Follow the gold arrow to the amber still to load shine, then deliver it to the
  blue drop to get paid. Hauling shine draws the law: break their line of sight
  to lose them. If they pin you down, you're busted.

TROUBLESHOOTING
  - Blank or black screen: turn on hardware acceleration in your browser settings
    and make sure the browser is up to date.
  - Your best haul is saved in this browser only.
