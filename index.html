HOW TO PLAY

  1. Follow the gold arrow to the ● still and drive into its ring to load shine.
  2. Deliver it to the ◆ drop to get paid.
  3. Hauling shine gets you tipped off. Heat stars send Feds and Temperance zealots after you.
